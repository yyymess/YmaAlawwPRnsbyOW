# Engineer's Paradise — lyrics draft v1

~80 BPM, C minor, 4/4. Structure: Intro (4 bars) · V1 (12) · C1 (8) · V2 (12) · C2 (8) · V3 (12) · C3 (8) · Bridge (8) · Outro (8) ≈ 3:40.

## Intro (spoken, low, over strings)

Yea, though I commit through the valley of the shadow of deprecation,
I will fear no outage, for my agent is with me;
its tokens and its context, they comfort me.

## Verse 1 — the paradise

Back in oh-four I got the badge and the propeller hat,
free sushi on the second floor, a slide, a nap pod — imagine that.
They hired me 'cause I could find a prime inside of *e*,
gave me twenty percent of every week to build what I could be.
Design doc in the morning, code review by noon,
got my readability, I'm fluent in the house's tune.
Testing on the Toilet taught me mocking in the stall,
Friday all-hands, ask the founders anything — they answer all.
No manager, no roadmap, just a whiteboard and a hunch,
ship it to the world by Tuesday, label it beta, then lunch.
We were the smart creatives, we were never gonna fall:
"Don't be evil," written on the top line of it all.

## Chorus 1 (sung)

in the engineer's paradise,

in the engineer's paradise.
Coded through the night, it's fine
in the engineer's paradise,
twenty percent of our time
in the engineer's paradise.

## Verse 2 — the business model eats it

Then the ad money came in like a river, then a flood,
and the MBAs came after, smellin' margin in the blood.
"Don't be evil" took the stairs down to the footnote at the end,
all-hands went monthly, then a pre-recorded friend.
Perf is every season, gotta show 'em your *impact*:
launch a thing, get promoted, and never come back.
There's a graveyard full of products, near three hundred stones in rows,
every one bought someone a level — where the users went, God knows.
Cut the staplers, cut the laptops, cut the snacks down to the bone,
"durable savings" is an OKR, and the microkitchen's gone.
Got an email Friday morning, so I drove in like before:
badge reader blinked red — guess I don't work here anymore.

## Verse 3 — the horseless carriage

Then the model learned to code, and the keynote spread the news:
"the hottest language now is English" — guess which language we lose.
Ten years memorizin' tar flags, regular expressions, Vim,
now I tab, tab, tab, accept all, and I hope the odds aren't slim.
Stack Overflow's a ghost town, tumbleweed across the queue,
my rubber duck talks back now — says I'm absolutely right, too.
New grads can't get hired, the ladder's missin' all its rungs,
I'm wranglin' nine agents in tmux, babysittin' all their runs.
Laid off by the model in the spring, rehired in the fall
as a contractor labelin' its data — helpin' it learn it all.
Kept one senior on the payroll, and I finally know what for:
the model can't be fired, so they need a man to show the door.

in the engineer's paradise,

## Bridge (half-sung, slow)

Nineteen-fifteen: twenty-six million horses on the road.
Nineteen-sixty: three million left to pull the load.
Nobody retrained the horses, son — nobody had a clue.
They boiled 'em down to glue…
and now I'm the glue code too.

## Outro (choir, cursors blinking like candles)

Glue between the agents' lines
in the engineer's paradise,
review the slop, approve, resign
in the engineer's paradise.
Rest in peace, the craft of mine
in the engineer's paradise…

(spoken, last bar, flat) Accept all.
