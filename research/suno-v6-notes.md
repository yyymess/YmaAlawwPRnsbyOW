# Suno v6 prompting notes (read 2026-10-06)

Sources: hookgenius.app/learn/suno-v6-guide · sunostyles.com/blog/suno-v6-settings · undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-prompts · undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts · undetectr.com/blog/suno-prompts-for-hip-hop · github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide

- **Variety rewrites the style prompt.** Suno's v6 FAQ: Variety works by "adjusting and updating your style prompts"; 0 keeps the tags as typed. Start at Variety 0.
- Starting sliders several guides agree on: Weirdness 20%, Style Influence 75%, Variety 0.
- **Negatives in the style box are read as things to include** ("no humming" puts humming in). Put unwanted things in Exclude, as categories, fewer than ten.
- v6 follows the style field literally; anything unspecified, it decides. v6 Pro rewards detail: each instrument as a sentence about its job, the singer with performance verbs.
- Order that users report working: Genre, Vocals, Drums, Guitars, Bass, other instruments, Arrangement/energy, Production, Ending.
- Section tags in the lyrics should be short and repeat the style field's words; contradictions get resolved by thinning things out.
- v6 "reads" dense lines at tempo instead of singing them (good for rap verses); short choruses with held last words get sung.
- The most reliable lever reported: an uploaded reference (even a rough phone recording) for cadence and inflection; timbre itself can't be prompted.
- Hip-hop formula: subgenre + BPM + drum character + bass + mood + vocal flow (+ reference). Boom-bap 85–95 BPM.
