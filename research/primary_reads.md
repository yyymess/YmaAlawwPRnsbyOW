# Primary reads (fetched and read in full)

## Steve Blank — "The Elves Leave Middle Earth – Sodas Are No Longer Free" (2009-12-21)
https://steveblank.com/2009/12/21/the-elves-leave-middle-earth-%E2%80%93-soda%E2%80%99s-are-no-longer-free/
- Scene: engineers clustered around the soda machine; sign: "Soda now 50 cents."
- Same moment: informal late-dinner reimbursement replaced by a formal expense-report system; "professional" managers hired over the teams, reportedly with more stock than the early engineers.
- The best engineers "looked up from their desks" and saw it was no longer the company they loved; within a month they drifted away.
- Thesis: a startup built to learn/discover/iterate metamorphoses into a company built to execute; "The sodas were just the wake-up call."
- Visual: a soda fridge with a new coin slot and a hand-lettered price sign; an expense-report form replacing a pizza receipt.

## Ed Zitron — "The Men Who Killed Google" (wheresyoured.at)
https://www.wheresyoured.at/the-men-who-killed-google/
- Feb 2019: the ads/finance side calls a "code yellow" over search revenue ("steady weakness in the daily numbers"); per Levy, "code yellow" is named after a former VP's yellow tank top.
- The head of search writes that search is "getting too close to the money" and that "growth is all that Google was thinking about"; notes queries could be raised "in user negative ways".
- 2019–2020: the ad label shrinks from a green badge to a small black "Ad" so ads look like results.
- 2020: the 20-year search veteran is moved to "SVP of Education"; the ads head becomes head of search. (Zitron's framing; characterizations of individuals are his.)
- Generic archetypes for the song (no names): the founding engineer moved to a do-nothing role; the revenue alarm called on the product; ads dressed as results; "the revenue engine of the company".
- Visual: a traffic-light "CODE YELLOW" siren on a dashboard; a green "Ad" badge fading into a tiny grey one.
